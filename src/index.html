So, what actually is super?

Superannuation, usually just called super, is money put aside during your working life to help pay for life after work. In Australia, employers generally have to contribute a percentage of eligible employees’ earnings into super, where the money is invested over time. It’s your money for the future, but it can be hard to know why you need it, who has it, how much you need and why are there so many different funds?
